PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how The Everything may collect, use, protect, and handle information when you use our website, create an account, save content, or contact us. Our goal is to keep the experience useful while respecting your privacy and being clear about how information is handled.

FIRST — How We Use Your Information

We use information only for purposes connected to operating and improving The Everything, including:

  • Creating, maintaining, and managing your account.
  • Remembering content you watched, saved, downloaded, liked, or interacted with when those features are available.
  • Responding to your requests, questions, support messages, and other inquiries.
  • Remembering useful preferences and improving the website's features, reliability, security, and overall experience.

SECOND — Cookies

The Everything may use cookies or similar browser technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, maintain sessions, improve performance, and help website features work correctly. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, but disabling some cookies may cause certain features to stop working properly. If we introduce additional analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies in the future, this policy may be updated accordingly.

THIRD — How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not intentionally disclose or leak account information. We take reasonable steps to protect your account and data. Information may only be shared when necessary to operate trusted services used by the website, comply with applicable law or a valid legal request, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the rights and security of The Everything and its users. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but protecting user information remains an important priority.

FOURTH — Children

The Everything is not designed or specifically directed toward children. Users who are below the minimum age required by the laws that apply to them should not create or use an account without any permission or supervision required by those laws. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising a child's internet use. If we become aware that personal information from a child was collected contrary to applicable law, we may take reasonable steps to remove it.

FIFTH — Your Privacy Rights

We respect your rights regarding your personal information. Depending on the features available and the laws that apply to you, you may be able to view or update your account information, request correction or deletion of certain data, ask what information is associated with your account, or contact us with a privacy concern. Some information may need to be retained when required for security, legal, technical, or legitimate operational reasons.

SIXTH — Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is not fixed forever. It may be updated as The Everything changes, introduces new features, uses new technologies, or changes the way information is handled. The latest version published on this page will apply, so we recommend reviewing this page from time to time for important updates.

SEVENTH — Support & Privacy Questions

Have a question about your privacy, your account information, or this Privacy Policy? Please visit our Support page and use the available support or contact options to reach us. We will review privacy-related questions and requests as reasonably as possible.
