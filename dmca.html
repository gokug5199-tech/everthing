DMCA & COPYRIGHT

Copyright & Content Removal

The Everything respects the rights of creators, artists, publishers, studios, photographers, designers, and other copyright owners. If you believe content available through the website infringes material that you own or are authorized to represent, you can send us a removal request for review.

Ownership Stays With Its Owner

Third-party anime, films, images, wallpapers, artwork, logos, characters, names, videos, and other materials remain the property of their respective owners. Their appearance on The Everything does not transfer ownership to us, and we do not claim rights over material that belongs to someone else.

Who Should Submit a Request?

A request should be submitted by the copyright owner, an authorized representative, or a person who has a legitimate legal basis to act for the rights holder. If you are reporting something, please make sure you can clearly explain your connection to the work.

What We Need From You

Please provide enough information for us to identify and review the material, including:

  • The work you believe is protected by copyright.
  • The exact page or link where the reported material appears.
  • Your name and, when relevant, the rights holder you represent.
  • A clear explanation of why you believe the use is unauthorized.
  • Any additional details that can help us verify the request.

What Happens After You Report It?

We will review the information provided and may check the reported page, request additional details, or take other reasonable steps to understand the issue. If action is appropriate, content may be removed, disabled, restricted, corrected, or otherwise addressed.

Please Use the Process Honestly

Do not use copyright reporting to target content simply because you dislike it, to impersonate another owner, or to submit knowingly false or misleading claims. Requests that are incomplete, abusive, fraudulent, or clearly unrelated to copyright may be rejected or left unprocessed.

Serious or Repeated Issues

Where appropriate, The Everything may take additional action regarding repeated copyright concerns connected to content, accounts, or site activity. The action taken will depend on the circumstances, the information available, and any applicable legal requirements.

Send a Copyright Request

To report a copyright issue, sign in and use our Support page. Include the exact link and all relevant details so the request can be reviewed as efficiently as possible.
